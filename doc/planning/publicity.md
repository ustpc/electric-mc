# Publicity: where Manuscript Central users gather

Research done 2026-10-16. Purpose: find places to announce Electric MC, recruit
pilot journals, and hear what editors want.

Short answer: **there is no open, public forum for Manuscript Central
(ScholarOne Manuscripts) users.** The vendor runs a client-only forum and
several events, and editors talk about it in professional-society venues.

## Vendor-run channels (Silverchair)

ScholarOne has been owned by Silverchair since the acquisition announced on
2024-10-28. All official community channels are for **publisher staff of
ScholarOne client publishers**, not for individual editors.

The "in-workflow collaboration tools" user testing group is the most relevant
to Electric MC: it suggests Silverchair is exploring something like our
per-manuscript chat. A journal that joins Electric MC's pilot could also join
that group and tell us what the vendor is planning.

### Social media (second pass, 2026-10-16)

No subreddit, Discord, Slack, or mailing list dedicated to Manuscript Central
users turned up.

- **Reddit:** the research tools can't reach Reddit directly, and web search
  doesn't index it well. No ScholarOne-specific threads were found. Someone
  with a browser should search r/AskAcademia, r/PhD, and r/academia by hand.
- **X / Twitter:** the visible ScholarOne activity is the vendor's own
  `@ScholarOneNews` account. No complaint threads turned up through search.
- **Bluesky:** Silverchair (`@silverchairnews.bsky.social`) and the Society for
  Scholarly Publishing (`@scholarlypub.bsky.social`) post there. Journals
  announce their moves to ScholarOne there (for example Annals of Botany,
  2025-02-14). No complaint threads were found, but Bluesky's search API
  wasn't reachable, so this is incomplete.
- **Mastodon (for example fediscience.org):** nothing found through web
  search. Mastodon full-text search needs a signed-in account.
- **Hacker News:** a handful of comments from 2013–2024 mention ScholarOne or
  Manuscript Central (pop-ups, mobile use, separate logins, fake reviews).
  They're cited in [Ideas from the web](Ideas%20from%20the%20web.md).
- **Academic rumour forums (Political Science Rumors, EJMR):** there are
  threads about ScholarOne, for example "Can editors see how often you log
  into ScholarOne?", but the site blocks automated reading.

The social channels are better for announcing Electric MC than for finding
editors' complaints. Editors complain mostly in blog comments and journal
instructions, not in public posts.

## Suggested approach

1. Recruit one or two pilot journals through their editorial offices,
   starting with journals whose editors already publish ScholarOne workarounds.
2. Join the Forum Hub (see below) and raise Electric MC there and in a
   Publisher Working Group.
3. Pitch a Scholarly Kitchen guest post or an ISMTE webinar once the pilot has
   results (for example, time-to-first-view before and after).
4. Position Electric MC as complementary to Silverchair's roadmap (see
   [ScholarOne release history.md](ScholarOne%20release%20history.md)), and
   check the publisher's contract terms before any public launch.

## Joining the Forum Hub

Simson is now on the staff of a ScholarOne client publisher, so they can join.

1. Go to [Sign in or request access](https://www.silverchair.com/s1m-client-user-pages-redirect/).
2. As a first-time user, fill in the request form with your name, your
   organization (the client publisher), and your work email. Complete the
   CAPTCHA, and tick the box confirming you work for a ScholarOne Manuscripts
   client. Access is granted to authenticated client staff, so expect a
   manual approval step.
3. Separately, ask the publisher's Silverchair **Customer Success Manager**
   (or account manager) to add you to the Future Focus webinar invitations,
   the Community Advocates program, and any relevant User Testing Group. The
   public pages say all three are arranged through them.

### What to look for once inside

- **Full release notes and Beta features.** The quarterly notes our tools
  couldn't read. Fill the gaps in
  [ScholarOne release history.md](ScholarOne%20release%20history.md).
- **Editor Gateway timeline.** When the new editor dashboards and redesigned
  manuscript pages reach your journals. Our restyling selectors will need to
  handle them.
- **"In-workflow collaboration tools" user testing group.** Silverchair named
  this as a 2026 testing topic. It is the vendor feature closest to our
  per-manuscript chat, so joining it would show what they plan and when.
- **API access.** How a client gets ScholarOne Web Services credentials, and
  whether `addNote`, `makeEditorAssignment`, and the read calls are available
  to your journals. With them, Electric MC could read role assignments
  instead of configuring them by hand, and could sync notes into ScholarOne.
- **Silverchair Universe partner program.** The route third-party tools use to
  integrate with ScholarOne (Prophy, Hum, Imagetwin). It may be the right
  long-term home for Electric MC.
- **Policy on browser extensions and client-side tools.** Anything in the
  client terms or support articles about tools that read ScholarOne pages.
  This is open question 6 in [Design Goals.md](Design%20Goals.md).
- **Other clients' workarounds.** Threads where staff describe spreadsheets,
  macros, or scripts they use alongside ScholarOne. These are pilot partners
  and evidence for [Ideas from the web](Ideas%20from%20the%20web.md).
- **Event dates.** The next ScholarOne Summit, Forum Connect, and Future
  Focus webinar.
